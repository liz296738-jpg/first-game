# CONSTELLATION RESONANCES

Resonances are not ordinary upgrades. They are rule-changing capstones unlocked when a run invests deeply enough in compatible Affinity families.

## Affinity families

- **Solar / 日耀** — burst, crit, ignition, pressure
- **Lunar / 月相** — guard, orbitals, control, sustain
- **Void / 虚空** — sacrifice, penetration, rupture, singularity
- **Aether / 以太** — movement, chaining, cooldown, spatial manipulation
- **Machine / 机械** — repeaters, drones, targeting, automation

A good Resonance must visibly alter how the player approaches combat. Flat bonuses alone do not qualify.

## Implemented Resonances

### 日冕复写 / Corona Repeater
Requirement: Solar 3 + Machine 2

Every fifth primary-weapon volley creates an additional pair of angled solar projectiles.

Design purpose:
- makes rate-of-fire builds develop a cadence;
- rewards Solar damage investment and Machine repetition;
- produces a visible moment of power without becoming constant screen noise.

### 月步护幕 / Moonstep Mantle
Requirement: Lunar 3 + Aether 2

When its internal cooldown is ready, Phase Dash generates a short-lived one-hit phase guard.

Design purpose:
- turns dash timing into a defensive resource decision;
- rewards movement-oriented builds without making the player permanently invulnerable;
- creates a clear visual state around the player.

### 黑星破裂 / Blackstar Rupture
Requirement: Void 3 + Solar 2

An enemy killed by a critical hit ruptures and damages nearby enemies.

Design purpose:
- makes crit builds change target priority;
- creates burst-clearing moments in dense formations;
- links Void rupture identity to Solar precision.

## Rules for future Resonances

1. A Resonance should be understandable in one sentence.
2. It must change a behavior, timing decision, targeting decision, resource loop, or spatial rule.
3. Its activation must have a readable audiovisual signature.
4. It should create or reinforce a build identity.
5. Avoid unconditional permanent multipliers as the primary effect.
6. Avoid effects that only matter against trivial enemies.
7. Avoid chain reactions without hard limits.
8. New Resonances should create cross-family reasons to make difficult upgrade choices.

## Candidate next Resonances

These are design candidates, not commitments.

### Event Horizon Choir
Void + Lunar

Orbitals periodically collapse inward, pulling nearby enemies before snapping back out.

### Ghost Assembly
Machine + Aether

Drones record a short segment of the player's firing pattern and replay it after a movement threshold.

### White Dwarf Heart
Solar + Lunar

Losing a shield compresses stored energy into a delayed radial counterattack.

### Null Meridian
Void + Aether

Dashing through enemies marks a spatial line; marked enemies take a second hit when the line collapses.

The next Resonances should be implemented only after current three have been played and tuned.
