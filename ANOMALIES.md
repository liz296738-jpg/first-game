# ANOMALIES / CURSED UPGRADES

Anomalies are deliberately dangerous offers. They exist to create memorable run pivots, not to become a second tier of strictly better upgrades.

## Offer cadence

Current prototype rule:
- first possible offer at level 4;
- then at levels divisible by 4;
- at most one anomaly replaces a normal card in that choice;
- each anomaly can only be accepted once per run;
- weapon-incompatible anomalies are filtered out.

This cadence is provisional and should be tuned from playtest data.

## Implemented

### 玻璃引擎 / Glass Engine
Universal.

**Gain:** damage ×1.70  
**Cost:** maximum HP ×0.68

Purpose: a clean high-risk damage pivot with an immediately understandable survivability cost.

### 红线契约 / Redline Covenant
Projectile weapons.

**Gain:** fire rate ×1.60, movement speed ×1.10  
**Cost:** dash cooldown ×1.45

Purpose: turns a safe mobility resource into a harder timing decision in exchange for aggressive tempo.

### 裂界齐射 / Fracture Barrage
Projectile weapons.

**Gain:** +2 projectiles  
**Cost:** damage ×0.78, wider spread

Purpose: turns precision into area coverage and changes how close the player wants to be.

### 断冕协议 / Broken Crown
Orbit weapons.

**Gain:** +2 orbit blades  
**Cost:** maximum HP ×0.78

Purpose: increases close-range output while making mistakes much more expensive.

### 内坍冕环 / Collapsed Halo
Orbit weapons.

**Gain:** orbit damage ×1.75  
**Cost:** orbit radius ×0.68

Purpose: forces the player deeper into danger to receive the damage benefit.

## Quality rules

1. The upside and cost must both be visible in one short sentence.
2. The cost must matter to moment-to-moment play, not be a fake downside.
3. An anomaly should meaningfully redirect a build.
4. Never hide irreversible costs in fine print.
5. Weapon-specific anomalies must be filtered before cards are shown.
6. Anomalies should remain rare enough that accepting one feels like an event.
7. Avoid currency or grind systems that pressure the player to accept them.

## Next questions for playtesting

- Are level 4/8/12 offers too predictable?
- Is Glass Engine survivable enough without becoming mandatory?
- Does Redline Covenant create interesting dash tension or only frustration?
- Does Collapsed Halo make the player enter a satisfying danger radius?
- Should declining an anomaly delay the next offer, or keep the fixed cadence?
