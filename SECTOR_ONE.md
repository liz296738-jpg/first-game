# SECTOR I — GLASS EXPANSE / 玻璃荒原

## Vertical-slice pacing

The first playable sector now follows an authored sequence instead of being only a survival timer.

### 0–20s — Establish the field
- ambient pressure + Encounter Director;
- learn the chosen Seed Weapon;
- first upgrade choices;
- no mandatory objective yet.

### ~20s — Mirror Relay / 镜面中继站
A world-space synchronization field appears away from the player.

Rule:
- remain inside the field to accumulate synchronization;
- leaving causes partial progress decay;
- completion rewards cores, XP and repair.

Purpose:
- breaks the default survivor habit of orbiting the safest empty space;
- forces the build to survive while holding a location;
- creates a meaningful positioning test before the boss.

### ~41s after relay completion — Falling Star / 坠星回收
A time-limited celestial object lands elsewhere in the arena.

Rule:
- reach it before the signal decays;
- successful pickup grants a core, XP and repair;
- failure converts the landing site into a dangerous Rift Zone.

Purpose:
- asks the player to choose a route through an already-active battlefield;
- failure changes the combat space instead of only removing a reward.

### ~65s after objective/event resolution — The Observatory / 观测者

The first authored boss gates sector completion.

Phase 1:
- orbiting movement around the player;
- single-axis scanning beam with long telegraph;
- periodic Rift pressure.

Phase 2 at 55% HP:
- outer-ring break visual;
- faster movement;
- perpendicular cross-scan;
- more frequent Rift pressure.

Boss death:
- active scans cancel immediately;
- large multi-ring collapse;
- extra core reward;
- sector unlocks and biome progression can continue.

## Enemy ecology

Current normal/special archetypes:
- Drone — direct chase;
- Swift — fast flank pressure;
- Brute — slow body blocker;
- Caster — ranged spacing pressure;
- Glass Moth — orbits, telegraphs, then commits to a dive;
- Rift Seeder — keeps distance and creates persistent danger zones.

## Elite modifiers

### Mirror
Periodically restores a one-hit mirror layer. The protected hit is strongly reduced and clearly telegraphed.

### Volatile
On death, leaves a telegraphed Rift Zone. Killing the elite changes the immediate space instead of ending its threat instantly.

## Readability rules

- every scan beam has a charge line before damage;
- every Rift has a warm-up;
- Glass Moth commits to a readable aim before diving;
- world objectives use cyan/gold reward language, never enemy-danger red;
- boss phase changes use a different silhouette and palette, not only more projectiles.
